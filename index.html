            Report / Feedback

            Abuse, something inappropriate, a bug, a problem with voice or chat, or an idea. Only the village administrators can read it, and it is kept until they deal with it.

            Request a poster competition

            In the poster hall. Once an administrator approves it, the hall runs a competition for exactly 24 hours: every poster pinned there stays up for the whole competition, even after its author leaves.
